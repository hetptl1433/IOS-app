Import a price dataset

Hourly or five-minute interval-start CSV.

Required: timestamp, price. Optional: day_ahead, published_at, day_ahead_available_at. Timestamps must include a UTC offset; prices are cents/kWh. Negative prices stay intact.

⌁

Fetch ComEd history

Public five-minute electricity prices.

Historical observations, not future forecasts.

The ComEd feed provides published five-minute prices. Hourly billing averages are calculated only from complete hours. Historical publication vintages may be unavailable.

A bounded date window avoids large requests. Download additional windows and combine them offline for longer research datasets.

Keep the experiment honest

We preserve price spikes, negative prices, and missing-data warnings. Incomplete billing hours are not silently filled. Synthetic datasets are always labeled.
